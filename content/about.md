👋🏽 Hello, and welcome to my website! My name is Pramana Saldin and I will be a PhD student at [UC-Berkeley](https://math.berkeley.edu/home) in the Fall. Before this, I recieved my B.S. in Mathematics from [UW-Madison](https://www.math.wisc.edu/) (my home state!) and took part in a study abroad semester at the National University of Singapore.

I am a 2025 [Barry Goldwater Scholar](https://goldwaterscholarship.gov/). I have conducted research for the [SMALL REU](https://math.williams.edu/small/) (number theory and probability), the [UW-Madison REU in Analysis](https://web.math.wisc.edu/analysisrtg/node/7), and the [Madison Experimental Mathematics Lab](https://mxm.math.wisc.edu/). My research interests are generally in algebra.
